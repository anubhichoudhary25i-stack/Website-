01 / INTRODUCTION BIO & PHILOSOPHY

Who is Anubhi?

A curious mind with a storyteller’s heart and an instinct for what makes people tick.

I’ve always been fascinated by the little things: a phrase that stays with you, a brand you recognise without seeing its logo, or a story that makes you feel understood without knowing why.

What makes one message forgettable and another impossible to ignore? Why do we connect with certain brands as if they were people?

Somewhere between these questions and my love for words, I discovered my fascination with marketing, branding, and communication.

To me, a brand is more than a name, a logo, or a clever tagline. It’s a feeling, a perception, a promise, and a story told consistently enough to be remembered. And marketing? It’s the art of understanding people, finding the right insight, and turning it into something that resonates.

I’m Anubhi, perpetually curious, occasionally overthinking a three-word headline, and always looking for the story behind the story. Because the most interesting part of any brand isn’t just what it says; it’s what makes people listen.

Anubhi Choudhary • Pune, India
