### Tips for new client logos:
- Recommended format: Transparent PNG or SVG (looks best on the off-white card wells).
- In the Google Sheet under the **`Clients`** tab, you can enter just the filename (e.g. `my_client_logo.png`) or `images/clients/my_client_logo.png`.
